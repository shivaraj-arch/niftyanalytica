What this does and does not measure

P&L is in index points, not rupees. This backtests the signal against the Nifty 50 index itself, not an option or futures position. Option prices do not exist at minute resolution in any public source, so an intraday option P&L cannot be computed honestly here.

A signal is read on a bar's close and the position opens at the next bar's open. Entering at the close of the bar that produced the signal is look-ahead, and inflates every result.

Candles are from this project's own store, 2017-04-03 onward, validated against Kite on 10,180 overlapping bars. Past behaviour is not a forecast.

Exit

If no strike is within the tolerance of the premium you asked for, that expiry is SKIPPED and counted in the warnings — it is not quietly replaced with the nearest thing. Asking for ₹150 at DTE 0 yields 8 trades in eight years, and you should see that rather than a fabricated sample.

0 disables a rule. With all three at 0 the position is held to expiry settlement.

Capital

For a long-only structure the capital is exact — the premium paid. Anything with a short leg needs SPAN + exposure margin, which is not in this data, so it is estimated from notional and labelled as an estimate. It is not a broker quote.
